# Featuring pitch

For the App Store editorial form (Promote your app), and for the developer
story field.

## One line

Lantern is a language model that lives on the phone: it works with no signal,
sends nothing anywhere, and shows you exactly what it costs to run.

## The pitch

Most AI apps are a text box in front of a server. Lantern is the opposite. It
downloads an open model once, then runs it on the iPhone's own chip with
Apple's MLX framework. Turn on airplane mode and nothing changes.

That makes it useful in the moments other assistants go dark: a car that will
not start on a quiet road, a trail at dusk, a menu in a language you do not
read, a station at midnight. Its safety personas answer from reviewed guides
built into the app and name the passage they used, because a small model's
memory is not something to hand a person in trouble.

It is also honest about the machine. Before you download, a green light tells
you what your phone can run. While it writes, you watch the rate, the memory
and the heat. A plain-language page explains why an iPhone can run this much
and no more, using the phone's own numbers. Settings shows what staying on
the phone has saved compared with a data centre.

Lantern uses Apple's frameworks the way they were meant to be used: MLX for
inference, Apple Intelligence as a second engine for comparison, Vision-class
models for photos, and a Stable Diffusion port for drawing, all on device.
It is open source.

## Why now

Phones can finally run useful models, and people are starting to ask where
their words go. Lantern answers with nothing leaves.
