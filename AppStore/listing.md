## Description (4000)

Lantern runs a language model entirely on your iPhone. There is no server, no account and no API key. After a one-time download, it works in airplane mode, on a plane, on a trail, in a tunnel, anywhere. Nothing you type or photograph ever leaves the phone.

WHAT IT DOES
• Answers questions, explains, rewrites, summarises and drafts, offline.
• Looks at photos: point it at a menu abroad, a warning label, a trail sign, a breadboard.
• Draws pictures from a description, on the phone, in a few seconds.
• Keeps chats for seven days, then deletes them. Delete any chat sooner with a swipe.

PERSONAS FOR WHEN THERE IS NO SIGNAL
Roadside helper, first aid guide, outdoors and survival, travel phrasebook, a calm companion for waiting it out, a day-out helper for lists and errands, a homework tutor, an electronics tutor, and a curious mind for the question you would have searched. The safety personas answer from reviewed guides built into the app and name the passage they used.

A GREEN LIGHT BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD
Lantern reads your phone's memory, free space and chip and tells you which models will run comfortably, which will be tight, and which will not run at all, before a byte is downloaded. Choose from small, fast models that run on any supported iPhone up to larger ones for Pro phones.

SEE WHAT IT COSTS
While a reply is written you can watch the rate, the memory in use and the phone's thermal state. Settings shows what staying on the phone has saved compared with a data centre. A plain-language page explains how it all works, using your phone's own numbers.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE, IF YOU HAVE IT
On phones with Apple Intelligence you can answer with Apple's built-in model too, compare the two side by side, and let Apple's model help keep long chats going.

PRIVACY BY CONSTRUCTION
Lantern has no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only network use is the one-time download of public model files. Everything else happens on your phone, and the code is open.

Models are open weights from Meta, Alibaba, Microsoft, HuggingFace and Stability AI, converted for Apple silicon by the MLX community. They can be wrong, and small models are wrong more often. Treat Lantern as a fast, private writing and explaining partner, and check anything that matters.

Requires an iPhone with 4 GB of memory or more (iPhone 11 or later). Larger models need 6 GB or 8 GB.
